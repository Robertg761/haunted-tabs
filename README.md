# Haunted Tabs

A browser extension that haunts your tab bar. Tabs you neglect slowly die, turn into ghosts, and come back to bother you until you deal with them.

Built solo for [Hackyard Yard #5: Haunted](https://hackyard.tech/yards/yard-5), Oct 19 to Oct 23, 2026.
