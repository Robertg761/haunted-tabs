# Hackyard Yard #5 rules

Source: [Yard #5 page](https://hackyard.tech/yards/yard-5) and [Rules and FAQ](https://hackyard.tech/faq). Read before every change.

## Theme: Haunted

Build something that haunts: it scares, surprises, or creeps into something real. It has to work on real stuff. A spooky page playing a canned demo does not count.

For this project: the haunting must happen to the user's real open tabs in a real browser.

## Safety lines (hard rules)

- Harmless.
- Opt-in. Nothing haunts until the user turns it on.
- Reversible. Every effect can be undone.
- On your own stuff only.
- Never delete data. No permanent tab closing, no history or bookmark deletion.
- Never spreads. No messaging, sharing, syncing to others, or self-propagation.
- Never touches anyone else's device.

## Timing

- Kickoff: Mon Oct 19, 18:00 UTC (3:30 PM NT).

## Builder

- Solo only.
- Any AI model is allowed. The model used must be declared on the submission.
